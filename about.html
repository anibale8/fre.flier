Alicia de Lucas Bautista

Architect and photographer based in Madrid.

Most of what you’ll find here are buildings I’ve visited and trips I’ve taken. This site is part of my personal research: a place where I keep evolving the way I look at the places I go, searching for my own way of capturing the feelings they convey.

That search has a name, fre.flier: a certain freedom in the way of looking, and what a trip leaves behind.
